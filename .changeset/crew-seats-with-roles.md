A crew member is a seat: role, kind, role card, host and lane policy, WIP limit; one seat belongs to one crew. A seat's role card leads the first message a wire carries into it in each run, and is not repeated on later laps. A conversation that sits in two crews keeps the earliest membership — the later one is removed when the app next opens, unless a wire in that crew is still armed on it.
