While a conversation compacts its context, every place that names its state now says "Compacting context…" instead of "Finished": the Needs-you card (under Working, with no Acknowledge), the header pill, the status bar, the sidebar and Mission Control badges, and the command center. A message you send while it compacts, or while the drill runs, is no longer refused: it waits in the queue ("Waits for compaction") and is sent once the compaction or the drill is done. The composer tells you this before you type. While the drill runs, the header shows its stage: `drill · sealing`, `drill · compacting`, `drill · resuming`.
