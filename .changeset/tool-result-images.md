'convergence': patch

The images a tool produces now show under its result, in a local Claude session and in a remote one. A screenshot, or a Read of a PNG, appears below the tool's output without opening it, and opens full size on a click. A remote session downloads them from its host in the background, a few at a time and never more than the host said they weigh, so the transcript never waits on them. Images that could not be shown read as "2 images left out", with the reason: too large, over the per-turn limit, or not downloaded from the host.
