The refusal a remote session gets when it has nowhere to work is written once
(MAR-2999).

The sentence that turns away a remote session with no stated work place lived in
two files, byte for byte: `REMOTE_SPAWN_PLACE_REQUIRED` in the shared spawn
spec, which the composer draft, the recipe normalizer and the crew YAML reader
all ask, and a literal of its own inside the session service's create door. Two
encodings of one fact only ever agree until someone edits one of them, and this
is exactly the kind of sentence that gets edited — a user reports the wording,
the wording changes in the place it was found, and the other half of the app
goes on saying the old thing with nothing anywhere to notice.

The create door now imports the shared constant. No refusal changed and no
session's fate changed; the two doors simply cannot drift apart any more. A test
puts both of them against that one constant rather than against a quoted string,
so what it pins is not the wording but that there is only one of it.
