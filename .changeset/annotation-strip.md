Annotations waiting to be sent now sit in one compact row instead of stacking up the screen.

When you answer several places in a long reply, each annotation used to take a full-width row above the composer, so fourteen of them pushed the conversation out of view. They now wait as small pills in a single row that scrolls sideways, with a count of how many there are, and the composer stays its normal height.

Click a pill (or focus it and press Enter) to open it and read, edit or remove that annotation; press Escape to close it again. Arrow keys move along the row. What gets sent is unchanged — every annotation, in the same order, with the same words.
