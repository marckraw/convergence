Codex's Fast switch can now be changed in an open conversation, like the model and effort: the change is saved on the conversation and its next turn runs at that speed (a turn already running keeps its speed). With Fast off, a local Codex conversation now always asks Codex for standard speed, instead of inheriting the account's own default — which on some plans is Fast — so the switch says what actually runs.
